Mirroxy is distributed as a binary-only application. Its source code is not part of this release.
Xray and sing-box are unmodified, independent upstream executables.
Xray source and release: https://github.com/XTLS/Xray-core/tree/v26.7.28
sing-box source and release: https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box/tree/v1.14.0

GeoIP/Geosite sources: https://github.com/Loyalsoldier/v2ray-rules-dat ; https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-geoip ; https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-geosite
CA certificates: https://curl.se/docs/caextract.html (Mozilla CA bundle).
